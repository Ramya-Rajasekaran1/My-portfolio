# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Hiring managers, recruiters, and design peers looking for a senior UX specialist. The context is high-stakes professional review where the user needs to quickly validate skills, design leadership, and project outcomes.

## Product Purpose
A premium personal portfolio website showcasing the design work, methodology, and design leadership of Ramya Rajasekaran. Success means demonstrating exceptional UX craft, end-to-end design leadership, and clear business/user outcomes to secure senior design roles or design consulting partnerships.

## Positioning
Outcome-led, research-backed storytelling of complex enterprise, SaaS, and civic tech solutions (such as B2B tracking quality dashboards, unified filtering systems, disaster preparedness civic tools, and AI inclusivity research) with human-centric and accessible implementation.

## Operating Context
Hiring managers and recruiters browsing on desktop and mobile web, often scanning rapidly, seeking high-quality case studies, measurable metrics, and clean, responsive interactions.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Built with React, Next.js, and Tailwind CSS.
- Forced Dark Mode layout matching Blush, Petal, and Ivory brand colors.
- Interactive accessibility features (Skip link, accessibility widget supporting dyslexia font, reader view, contrast, and spacing controls).
- Responsive layouts showing detailed dashboard case studies and research telemetry.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Ramya Rajasekaran
- Voice: Professional, authentic, human, outcome-focused.
- Aesthetic Direction: Forced dark mode, Blush/Petal/Ivory palette, authentic/handcrafted elements (explicitly avoiding generic AI-default gradients, glowing borders, and templates).

## Evidence on Hand
Case studies:
- **Unified Filters**: Siloed UX consolidated into a unified filtering experience for FourKites B2B SaaS Enterprise Platform (2022).
- **SafeHome**: Earthquake & disaster preparedness for San Francisco (2025).
- **Gen AI Inclusivity**: Research paper presented at IEEE and IndiaHCI exploring impact of AI on inclusivity and universal design standards (2025).
- **Tracking Quality Dashboard**: Enterprise supply chain dashboard visibility and telemetry.

## Product Principles
1. **Authenticity over Slop**: Avoid generic, copy-paste AI design patterns. Every element should feel intentional, tailored, and unique.
2. **Outcome-Led Storytelling**: Every case study must culminate in a clear success story or metric.
3. **Accessible by Default**: Ensure every interaction, color contrast, and font accommodates all users natively, supported by interactive accessibility toggles.
4. **Fluid Motion**: Use purposeful, organic animations to guide scanning, avoiding rigid transitions.
5. **Professional Polish**: High-fidelity execution in typography, spacing, and visual hierarchy.
